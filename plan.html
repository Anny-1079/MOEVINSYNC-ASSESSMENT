Putting it into practice

Rollout plan

Start at 1,500 seats, measure for eight weeks, then set the final number from what people actually do. Booking and check-in data in WorkInSync supplies the evidence.

Four phases

Weeks 0 to 2
Baseline

Know the starting point

  • Confirm headcount, role mix and public holidays for each site.
  • Pull any badge or Wi-Fi data to see current attendance by weekday.
  • Agree the policy with HR: 3 days a week, team anchor days, no single mandated day.
  • Load floors, zones and desks for each office into the platform.
Weeks 3 to 10
Pilot

Run it with caps and check-in

  • Set a daily booking cap at about 90% of desks on each floor.
  • Require check-in and release desks unclaimed after a set time.
  • Publish a weekly report of occupancy, denials and no-shows per office.
  • Adjust anchor days where one weekday is running hot.
Weeks 11 to 12
Set the number

Replace the assumption with data

  • Calculate the real peak factor per site from eight weeks of bookings.
  • Fix the final seat count and any lease decisions with each site head.
  • Run the first satisfaction survey.
Every quarter
Right-size

Keep the estate matched to use

  • Review the KPI scorecard with the facilities head.
  • Release, sublet or add zones according to the rules on the KPI page.
  • Re-check the attendance pattern, since a drift to 4 days changes everything.

Platform settings I would start with

Booking rules

  • Daily cap per floor at about 90% of desks
  • Bookings open up to 2 weeks ahead
  • Check-in required, auto-release of unclaimed desks

Team features

  • Anchor days per team, staggered across the week
  • Neighbourhood zones so teams sit together
  • Desk booking linked to meal pre-booking

Reporting

  • Occupancy by day, floor and office
  • Denial rate and no-show rate
  • Weekly export to site leads

Per-country setup

  • Local holidays and weekend pattern
  • Occupancy limit per building
  • Local language and support hours

Risks and what I would do about them

Everyone picks Tuesday to ThursdayMonday and Friday under 50% while mid-week is fullAssign anchor days by team and cap bookings per day
Booked desks left emptyCheck-in rate under 85%Shorter release window, reminders on the booking day
Sites behave differentlyOne office well above or below the othersReview each site against its own pattern before copying a fix
Booking and badge data disagreePlatform shows far fewer people than turnstilesAudit adoption, train teams, reconcile weekly during the pilot
Vendor service differs by countryTicket close time varies widely between sitesCommon service levels in each vendor contract and a monthly review

Decisions I would ask leadership for

  • Confirm the 3-day policy and whether anchor days may differ by team.
  • Agree that booking caps and check-in are enforced from day one of the pilot.
  • Allow the final seat count to change after eight weeks, and for leases to follow it.
  • Name one owner per site who is accountable for the weekly report.
