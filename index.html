Seating 2,000 people who come in three days a week

As facility manager of three offices in three countries, I would plan for 1,500 seats. The true range is 1,200 to 2,000, and where the number lands depends on how evenly people spread across the week.

See the reasoning KPI scorecard

The answer

Minimum Plan Maximum 1,200 1,500 2,000
Minimum 1,200

2,000 × 3 ÷ 5. The average weekday, if attendance were spread perfectly evenly.

Recommended 1,500

Mid-week clustering makes the busiest day about 1.25× the average. That is 75% of headcount.

Maximum 2,000

Everyone chooses the same three days. Seats equal headcount.

In short

  • The floor is 1,200 desks and it only holds if every weekday carries the same crowd. That needs booking caps and team anchor days.
  • The ceiling is 2,000 desks and it happens when a company simply says "come in Tuesday to Thursday".
  • I plan for 1,500, then replace the 1.25× assumption with real peak-day data after an eight-week pilot.
  • Sites are sized one by one. Different public holidays, weekends and time zones mean one office cannot lend desks to another.
  • A good Facilities Team is judged on three things together: desks people can actually get, money not spent on empty space, and a safe, clean place they like to work in.
